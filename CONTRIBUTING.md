# Contributing

This repository currently publishes a research and architecture artifact.

Before submitting substantive code, design, policy, or documentation:

1. Open an issue describing the proposed contribution.
2. Confirm that the project steward is accepting that contribution category.
3. Do not include confidential information, restricted data, taxpayer
   identifiers, identity documents, credentials, private legal work product,
   or third-party material you do not have the right to submit.

External contributions may not be merged until the project adopts a
counsel-approved contributor agreement, provenance process, and outbound
license. A pull request by itself does not create compensation, equity,
royalties, ownership of the project, or entitlement under a future program.
