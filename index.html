GO WITH CHANGES U.S. PILOT NO TOKEN

A mind map for rights, rewards, and proof

Open code.
Earned upside.
Clean chain of title.

The workable model is not a magical Apache clause. It is a stack of separate instruments: code license, contributor grant, compensation plan, evidence ledger, human review, and regulated payout.

Architecture verdict

Build the pilot. Defer the token.

Start with one public repo, a U.S.-only invited cohort, retained contributor copyright, a commercial CLA, shadow scoring, signed Merkle checkpoints, and capped cash awards.

01 · The license fork

One promise has to lead.

True OSI open source and mandatory enterprise payment are different models. Pick the controlling promise before accepting outside contributions.

Apache answer

Apache-2.0 gives broad royalty-free rights. It does not give contributors future compensation or project ownership.

AGPL-3.0 plus a commercial alternative

Anyone may use the core without paying if they comply with AGPL. Enterprises can buy proprietary terms, warranties, hosting, support, or indemnity.

OSI statusYes
Forced paymentNo
Best whenThe same core code is the product
Honest market label

Open source with a paid commercial alternative

A broad contributor agreement is required to issue commercial alternatives.
02 · Operating mind map

Six systems, one contributor promise.

Select a branch to inspect the contract boundary. No single node is allowed to imply rights created by another.

Selected branch · rights

The code license governs users, not contributor pay.

Choose AGPL plus a commercial alternative if true open source leads. Choose BSL with a narrow free-use grant if enterprise payment leads. The compensation promise belongs in a separate signed plan.

03 · Evidence to payment

A receipt is not a payable.

Contribution merit and accounting liability stay separate until legal, tax, budget, and human approval gates are complete.

01

Agreement

Versioned contributor grant and compensation-plan enrollment.

02

Accepted work

Repository event, technical validation, and immutable evidence snapshot.

03

Final score

Deterministic rubric, independent reviews, policy digest, and appeal window.

04

Declared pool

Fixed U.S.-dollar budget with caps, eligibility, and documented reason codes.

05

Award notice

Legal, tax, sanctions, budget, and dual approval create a payable amount.

06

Reconciled cash

Fiat payout, information reporting, accounting record, and signed receipt.

Hard boundary: `final_score`, `award_weight`, and `approved_amount` are different fields. Points are nontransferable and carry no automatic redemption, equity, royalty, governance, or token promise.
04 · Red lines

What cryptography cannot fix.

These are product boundaries, not fine print. A disclaimer cannot repair a misleading claim or an unlawful instrument.

Do not launch

Project profit token

Transferable or profit-linked contribution tokens create material securities, tax, custody, market, and transmission risk.

Do not publish

Identity on chain

SSNs, EINs, tax forms, identity documents, bank details, wallets, and sanctions results stay encrypted and off ledger.

Do not promise

Fractional copyright

Shared repository title complicates relicensing, enforcement, acquisitions, inheritance, exits, and enterprise diligence.

Scope precisely

“CPA reviewed”

Preparation, compilation, review, audit, and agreed-upon procedures have different standards and assurance levels.

Use as evidence

DocuSeal

E-sign supports execution evidence. It does not cure authority, consent, drafting, worker classification, or illegality.

Separate engagement

Law-firm partner

State rules control fee sharing, referrals, conflicts, professional independence, privilege, confidentiality, and UPL.

05 · Maturity path

Prove fairness before upside.

The system earns the right to handle money only after deterministic replay, privacy, appeal, and reconciliation controls work.

Phase 0

Legal freeze

Entity, license, CLA, plan, privacy, free-tier terms, enterprise contracts, threat model, and vendor review.

Phase 1

Shadow score

One repo, invited cohort, DocuSeal, deterministic scoring, appeals, receipts, and no cash promise.

Phase 2

U.S. cash pilot

Verified cohort, fixed pool, signed award notices, private tax onboarding, ACH, and full reconciliation.

Phase 3

Enterprise beta

Free-tier entitlements, commercial contracts, release chain of title, billing, and board-approved pool.

Later gates

International and token

Country matrix and fiat first. Any token is a separate counsel-led securities and security project.
